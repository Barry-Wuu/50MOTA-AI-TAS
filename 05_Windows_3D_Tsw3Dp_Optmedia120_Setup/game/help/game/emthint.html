Welcome to the room of lava. I am Lucky Gold.

It is secret where I am, but you can find me by using an item.
You get twice as much gold when you beat monsters, if you have me(So you had better find me early!)
The old person of the 23rd floor tells you a hint about me.
I am only one.
I am lonely...

I shall explain swords and shields in 'Tower of the Sorcerer'.

This is the Iron Sword.
This is the first sword you must find in the game.
There is one on the 6th floor.

This is the Iron Shield.
There is on the 9th floor.
You can take it easily if you notice the loophole.

This is the Silver Sword.
You must get this sword in the second area to use against the immortal force.

This is the Silver Shield.
There is one on the 11th floor of the army corps of immortal force.
But don't take it right away, you had better go to get the Silver Sword first.

This is the Knight Sword.
You can get it by the area of the Golden Knight corps(31st-40th floor).
Pay close attention when you take it because a gang of knights are defending it.
You had better talk to the old person who is near it first.

This is the Knight Shield.
You can get it by the block of the Golden Knight corps(31st-40th floor)...
Don't just walk up and take it...
Ask the merchant first.

This is the Holy Sword.
This is in the area of the Magic Sergeant Zeno(41st-49th floor)...
A normal key won't open this door.
You will see how to open the door to this room when you find a hiding monster.

This is the Holy Shield.
This is in the area of the Magic Sergeant Zeno(41st-49th floor)...
Be careful of the magic of witches.

This is the Sacred Sword.
The Hero had it when he entered into the Tower for the first time...
This sword was taken at the 3rd floor and was sealed in the room of the lava of the 13th floor.
An item is necessary to open this seal.

This is the Sacred Shield.
The Hero had it when he entered into the Tower for the first time...
This Shield was hidden in the floor of illusion.
You had better be careful of your energy level, because the monster who is defending this shield is really very strong.
