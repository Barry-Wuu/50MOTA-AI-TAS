[High Score information in Tower of the Sorcerer]   
This is an attempt to publish the world's high scores from "Tower of the Sorcerer" .  

[How to take part]
Just send the Score data file to Oz by e-mail.

  Option1.dat is the 10th floor.
  Option2.dat is the 20th floor.
  Option3.dat is the 25th floor.
  Option4.dat is the 40th floor.
  Option5.dat is the 49th floor   

Oz's Email Address is furano@po2.across.or.jp
Please use the subject: "Ranking of Tower of the Sorcerer".
And please include your name in the E-mail.

High scores are displayed at Oz's Homepage.
